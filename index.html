Focus Areas

Research Directions

My work combines established bioinformatics and computational genomics problems with modern machine-learning methods.

Bioinformatics & Computational Genomics

Developing computational methods for biological sequence analysis, genomic prediction, variant interpretation, phylogenomics, and other problems involving genomic and evolutionary data.

Single-Cell & Spatial Genomics

Studying disease-associated cellular variation, cell-state representation, spatial transcriptomics, cell-type annotation, and integration of single-cell and spatial biological data.

Machine Learning for Biology

Investigating biological foundation models, representation learning, model adaptation, interpretability, robustness, and controllability for biological and genomic machine-learning systems.
